# Tails & Trails — Open Pet-Care Image Archive

Tails & Trails is an open-media archive preserving companion-animal and pet-care photography from Ericeira, Portugal.

## Open companion-animal photography from Ericeira

The collection contains **173 openly licensed photographs** of dogs, cats and a mouse, with bilingual captions, file-level attribution and provenance records. Public copies exclude original camera EXIF, device identifiers and exact capture-location metadata.

**[Browse all 173 photographs in the open image catalogue](https://tails-and-trails.github.io/)**

The [GitHub archive registry](https://github.com/tails-and-trails/tailsandtrails) remains the canonical source for files, hashes, attribution records and preservation metadata.

## Reuse and attribution

The photographs are available under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

When reusing a photograph, credit **Tails & Trails Archive** and preserve its supplied file-level attribution.
